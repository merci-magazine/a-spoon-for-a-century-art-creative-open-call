# A Spoon for a Century | 一匙に託すもの | 一湯匙能承載的

**Art & Creative Open Call 2026 — An open creative initiative by Merci Magazine**

**Official information and submission form:** https://www.merci-magazine.com/art-creative-open-call

> What can a single spoon hold across a lifetime?  
> A memory, a feeling, a story, an unfinished dream, or a vision still waiting to be realised.

## About the initiative

*A Spoon for a Century* is an international art and creative open call initiated by **Merci Magazine** and curated by **Peggy Lui**, Founder and Editor-in-Chief of Merci Magazine. Participants from different cities, cultures and backgrounds are invited to use one spoon as the starting point for an individual creative response to memory, feeling, personal experience and ideas for the future.

**This is not a competition.** There are no rankings or prizes. Initial ideas are selected through a curatorial process.

## Creative scope

A work may take the form of writing, an object, a miniature scene, sculpture, textiles, art, design, fashion, architecture, food culture or another form of expression. Each work must use **one spoon as an integral, central element**. The work may extend within, above, around or outward from the spoon; it does **not** have to fit inside its bowl.

Reference dimensions for public submissions: approximately **12.5–13 cm** long and **4–4.2 cm** bowl width. An approximate **20 × 20 cm** display footprint is envisaged; final display requirements have not yet been announced.

## Exhibitions and cultural exchange

The project aspires to develop across creative disciplines and potentially reach audiences in different cities. Details of venues, dates and future programmes should be checked on the official website. Do not interpret ambitions as confirmed exhibition bookings.

## Art, life and social purpose

The initiative also aims to support **brain aneurysm medical research, public awareness and education, and patient support**. Any surplus available **after exhibition production, venue, dismantling and related operating costs** is intended for these charitable purposes. **The beneficiary organisation and donation arrangements have not yet been confirmed.** This is not a claim that every participation fee will be donated.

## Invited creative participants

Merci Magazine is developing personal invitations for creative practitioners who may contribute artistic interpretations and help encourage broader participation and fundraising. Invitations, roles and any materials specifically arranged for invited participants are subject to individual confirmation; they are **not** the standard public open-call requirements. No individual or brand should be presented as a participant or supporter before acceptance.
